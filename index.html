    CLOSE
    • A trading month that balances before anyone asks for the numbers.
    • A price written on a phone and sent before the conversation ends.

    • A payment that settles where anyone can check it.

    HOLD
    • Stock that knows what is still in the yard, and what is out on hire.
    • An invoice that follows the gear, without a second set of books.
    • Jobs, materials, and crew, with the cost kept on the work.
    • A site that can say what it spent without calling the office.
  3. 03

    Clinic

    CARE
    • One appointment, visible from the desk and from the phone in a pocket.
    • A trial written down while it is still happening.
    • A dispensary that keeps going when the network drops.
    • Open shifts filled before the gap becomes the day.
  4. 04

    Signal

    SENSE
    • A handset rewritten so another machine hears it.
    • A camera standing in for a pedal no one can see.
    • Sound, caught — a hum turned into notes, a track split into parts.
    • A line measured for speed, delay, and the stall between them.
  5. 05

    Local

    ROOM
    • Picture cut to a beat, and it never leaves the tablet.
    • The last minute of a match, kept on a single key.
    • A file that crosses from the desk to a pocket on the same network.
    • A machine made faster, with the old state kept so it can return.
  6. 06

    Table

    PLAY
    • Four short games, and one score the room shares.
    • A story run inside a chat, with roles, and a night.
    • A family calling the matches before they are played.
    • Ranks pulled out of a photograph of a board.
